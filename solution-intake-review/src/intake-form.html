How routing works
Hard escalation flags force Option C. Otherwise Option B is selected when personal data, provider hosting, regulated contexts, or non-trivial system impact exists. Option A is reserved for bounded, low-risk, client-operated solutions.

3) Processing options (for consideration)

Use these as a menu of rigor levels. The intake engine recommends one.

Option A — Rapid Triage

Goal: fast feasibility + deal hygiene + pattern match.
Best for: client-operated, advice-only, reads-only, non-sensitive.
Target SLA1–2 business days
ArtifactsIntake form + 1-page summary (auto-generated)
DecisionPre-cleared or escalate to Option B/C

Option B — Standard Intake

Goal: scoped delta reviews + short Evidence Pack.
Best for: regulated pilots, personal data, limited provider hosting, gated actions.
Target SLA5 business days to decision
ArtifactsShort Evidence Pack: data map, hosting boundary, oversight model, controls summary
DecisionProceed with constraints, or escalate to Option C

Option C — Full Proposal

Goal: high assurance for production-grade or provider-run solutions.
Best for: government/critical, managed service, automatic decisions, write/execute in core systems.
Target SLA2–6+ weeks depending on scope
ArtifactsFull Evidence Pack: architecture, resilience, governance, contracts, runbook, risk acceptance
DecisionFormal approvals + recorded acceptance + operating constraints

Typical workstreams by review

  • Security/Architecture: boundary, access controls, threat model, logging
  • Privacy/Data Protection: data roles, DPIA, residency/transfer, retention
  • AI Governance: autonomy limits, explainability, monitoring/drift, training
  • Contract/Legal: liability, SLAs, permitted use, audit rights, subcontractors
  • Ops/Resilience: incident response, change control, DR, support model
